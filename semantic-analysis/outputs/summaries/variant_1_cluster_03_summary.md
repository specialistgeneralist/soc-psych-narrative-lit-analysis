**Proposed cluster label:** Narratives as Ideological Frameworks for Significance-Seeking and Action

**Synthesis paragraph:** Across these excerpts, a consistent theoretical construct emerges: narratives are socially validated, ideological meaning-making frameworks that connect individual psychological needs—particularly the need for personal significance—to collective belief systems and prescribed behavioral pathways. They function simultaneously as interpretive structures (explaining grievances, attributing causality, differentiating ingroups from outgroups) and as prescriptive structures (legitimizing specific actions, including violence, as appropriate means to valued ends). The construct is applied most consistently to contexts of radicalization and extremism, though prosocial orientations are acknowledged.
